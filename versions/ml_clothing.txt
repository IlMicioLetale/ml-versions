3.2.0

[NEW FEATURES]
- Dropped items show on the ground as their own 3D prop instead of the drop bag.
- Before dropping, players can aim, turn and place the item where they want.
- Hold the right mouse button while placing to look around.

[IMPROVEMENTS]
- The trunk placement key bar is smaller and fits on two lines.
- The trunk placement outline stays green while you move the item and turns red only where it cannot go.
- New look for the lock on locked loot crates.
- Loot containers no longer have stripes.
- The heavy plate carrier now shows 2 plate slots.
