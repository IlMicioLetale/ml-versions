1.0.6

[NEW FEATURES]
- Biofuel is now a full fuel type, with its own bottle and conversion kit.
- Players can place solar panels that charge in daylight.
- No-loot zones can block siphoning, wreck searches and pump scavenging.
- Six EV chargers now come on the map with blips.

[IMPROVEMENTS]
- The jerry can sits in the hand and pours into the fuel cap.
- EV charging takes real time and you can walk away from the car.
- The fuel panel and vehicle inspection now follow your language.
