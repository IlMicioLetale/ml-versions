1.2.0

[NEW FEATURES]
- Painkillers and morphine now really stop the pain, with a timer in the medical panel.
- New doseGroup on items: items in the same group share one dose counter.
- Doses survive a relog, so reconnecting no longer clears an overdose.
- New GetMedicalData export to feed your own HUD.
- Base Building support: no tents in other people's bases, base beds on the death screen.

[IMPROVEMENTS]
- The medical panel shows a Medication in system row with doses and time left.
- 3 new locale lines, in all 9 languages.

[BUG FIXES]
- Overdose hit the medic instead of the patient.
- Overdose did not take the item, so the blur effect was free.
- Painkiller items did nothing at all.
- Blood regen boosts never worked.
- The tent was free if the item removal failed.
