3.0.1
[NEW FEATURES]
- Added support for clothing collections from GTA DLCs and custom clothing packs.
- Added bag capacity, armor type and extra clothing component settings for each collection.
[IMPROVEMENTS]
- Clothing keeps its collection data when appearance is updated.
- Added the ExchangeItems server export for other scripts. Items are removed and given together; a rejected exchange leaves the inventory unchanged.
- ML Bridge is now listed as a required inventory dependency.
[BUG FIXES]
- Fixed double-click, Use and Give selecting a weapon from the first pocket slots.
- Fixed transfers and item updates in equipped bags.
- Fixed the equipment panel not reopening after closing an equipped inventory.
