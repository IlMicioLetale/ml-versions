3.0.1
[NEW FEATURES]
- Added optional ground placement for dropped items, including items in equipped bags.
- Added clothing collections, with bag sizes, armor settings and clothing requirements.
- Added ExchangeItems for server scripts to remove and give items in one operation.
[IMPROVEMENTS]
- Clothing keeps its collection data when appearance is updated.
- ML Bridge is now listed as a required inventory dependency.
[BUG FIXES]
- Fixed double-click, Use and Give selecting a weapon from the first pocket slots.
- Fixed transfers and item updates in equipped bags.
- Fixed the equipment panel not reopening after closing an equipped inventory.
- Fixed drops being blocked when an older item placement resource is running.
