3.2.2

[NEW FEATURES]
- Dropped items now show as a 3D prop on the ground.
- You can aim, rotate and place the item where you want.
- You can turn off the weapon panel and use slots 1 and 2 as pockets.
- You can hide rarity everywhere.

[IMPROVEMENTS]
- Vans now check the wheel arches when you place items in 3D.
- Workbench and stash props now sit on the ground when you place them.
- Crafting now shows a progress bar.
