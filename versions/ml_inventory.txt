3.1.1

[NEW FEATURES]
- Added a crafting menu with recipe previews, search, categories and a filter for craftable recipes.
- Ingredients, tool wear, crafting time and output amount are shown before crafting.
- Added batch crafting with a quantity selector and a Max button.
- Added a workbench editor to the admin panel with recipes, reusable tools, duplication, locations, props, job restrictions and map blips.
- Added a shop menu with search, categories, stock limits, rank requirements and a cart.
- Shops can take cash, bank or both, per shop or per product.
- Item currencies such as black money show with their image in shops.
- Added 3D trunk placement, off by default.
- Works with car trunks, pickup beds, vans and box trucks with rear doors.
- Vans and trucks open their rear doors by themselves during placement.
- Items sit on the cargo floor and can be stacked on top of other placed items.
- Added a trunk camera with rotate, tilt, roll and straighten controls.
- Placed items are visible to every nearby player and keep their position and rotation.
- Each item can use its own prop, with presets for weapons, ammo and common items, other items use a box.
- Trunks of vehicles without an owner are saved too, and cleaned up after some days without use.
- Added temporary job uniforms that restore civilian clothing and keep equipped bags, armor and plate health.
- Clothing can be a loot reward in ML scripts that support it, with a photo of every model.
- Added clothing image exports for other resources.
- Added an option to remove the weapons panel, slots 1 and 2 become the first two pocket cells.
- Added an option to hide item rarity everywhere.

[IMPROVEMENTS]
- Reworked the crafting and shop menus to match the inventory panels.
- All panel headers now have the same size.
- Panels placed under or next to another panel now follow it when you change the UI scale.
- Shop purchases now stack on items you already have.
- Trunk placement always uses the inventory's own engine, so it works the same with or without ML Item Throwing.
- The trunk placement preview is only green or red, and stays visible while you rotate it.
- Items move more smoothly while you place them in a trunk.
- Trunk placement controls now use the same key bar as the other ML scripts.
- The Place All Items setting is hidden when ML Item Throwing is not installed.
- Hotkeys 1 and 2 now use the weapon slots, the quick wheel uses 3 to 7.
- Items that cannot stack past their limit are now blocked before placement.
- Rarity style changes apply right away.
- Changes to item degradation settings now apply to existing items.
- The idle camera no longer starts while the inventory is open.
