1.2.0

[NEW FEATURES]
- Added a clothing reward to the loot editor: pick a fixed piece or a random piece from a series, with a photo of every model (needs ML Inventory)
- Looting zone props and world props can now advance battlepass objectives
- Added server exports to read zone data, list zones, check if a player is in a zone, refresh zone loot and turn a zone on or off

[BUG FIXES]
- Fixed world props and prop categories set as active being treated as inactive, so world props could not be looted
- Saving or deleting a world prop or prop category now works right away, without a restart
- Fixed players collecting zone loot from another routing bucket, such as an apartment or instance on top of the zone
